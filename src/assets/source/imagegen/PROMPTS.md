# Image-generation prompts

Built-in image generation was used with the settlement growth and mouse-first build-menu concepts
as style-only references.

All sources request unusually polished late-era Commodore 64 pixel art, chunky square pixels, hard
dark outlines, no antialiasing, no text, and a limited palette derived from the documented Ashfall
colors. Sprite sources use a flat `#ff00ff` chroma-key background.

## Ground

Seamless top-down ash-wasteland ground: dry ochre soil, tiny charcoal pebbles, sparse olive scrub,
occasional rust grit, evenly distributed with no large focal object.

## Tarp shelter

A fragile makeshift shelter with patched triangular tarp, support poles, bedroll, and one supply
crate, viewed in three-quarter top-down orthographic perspective.

## Water collector

An improvised angled metal catchment sheet draining into two cyan-blue barrels on rough timber
braces, viewed in three-quarter top-down orthographic perspective.

## Scrap cache

An organized salvage pile of corrugated sheets, gears, pipes, and a small rusted cart, viewed in
three-quarter top-down orthographic perspective.

## Permanent Hub

A compact durable community hall of reinforced corrugated metal and reclaimed masonry with a
central doorway, short radio mast, warm occupied windows, and notice board. It is sturdier than
makeshift structures but still belongs to the early post-apocalyptic Camp.
